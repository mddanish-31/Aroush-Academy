# Phase 3 central fee collection, local implementation

This records the 15 November 2026 local work. It is not approval of any fee schedule, receiving account, refund policy, statutory receipt format or production migration. Earlier recurring-billing and teacher-collection proposals in `docs/backend/schema-plan.md` do not govern this phase. The portal has no gateway, webhook, bank credentials or proof-image storage.

## Migration and ledger

`drizzle/0008_furry_prodigy.sql` adds obligations, adjustments, UPI submissions, payments, allocations and receipts, with indexed foreign keys, positive paise checks and unique request keys. Immutable-posting triggers reject updates and deletes of obligations, adjustments, payments, allocations and receipts. The existing `settings` table stores one central payee configuration, and `number_sequences` issues atomic internal `FEE-YYYY-NNNNNN` receipt numbers. The sequence is provisional, not a claimed statutory format. No receiving details or financial obligations are seeded. The local PostgreSQL harness applies migration 0008 to a disposable loopback-only cluster and removes it afterward. No hosted database was migrated.

An obligation snapshots the explicit admin-entered amount, period and due date against a real enrollment and student. A unique enrollment-period pair and unique request key reject duplicate creation. Amounts are positive integer paise, up to 100000000 per action. Balance equals original obligation minus posted allocations minus discounts and waivers. Discounts and waivers require an admin-entered reason and cannot exceed the remaining balance. They are immutable and audited. There is no refund or reversal operation. Payment attempts above the locked balance fail without posting.

Cash requires a configured payee name. UPI instructions, QR and submissions require a configured validated UPI ID and payee name. The administrator provides the approved name, optional `handle@provider` UPI ID and optional `+91` payment phone; a password check and version check precede a change. Before and after values are written to the restricted audit log in the same transaction. Payers see only the configured receiving name, UPI ID and optional payment phone. These are necessarily visible to anyone instructed to pay. QR SVG and UPI deep links derive the destination from this configuration and use the server-calculated balance and selected amount. The desktop test inspects links; it does not assert that a mobile UPI app actually executed a transfer.

Cash posting locks the obligation and commits one payment, one full allocation, one receipt, a sequence increment and an audit event together. UPI submissions remain pending and do not reduce dues. Students and verified parents may submit a reference and date, but not a proof image. Admin review requires an explicit settlement confirmation or a rejection reason. Review locks the obligation and submission, enforces a pending-only transition, and rechecks the balance. Normalized duplicate references are flagged in the admin queue; a unique index on posted UPI references prevents a second approval even across different obligations. A rejected reference never reduces dues. A return from a UPI app or a screenshot is not proof of settlement. Financial corrections currently use an additional reasoned adjustment only while a positive balance remains; actual reversal and external refund handling require an owner policy.

## API and permissions

All fee responses use `Cache-Control: private, no-store`; writes require same-origin requests and server sessions. Fees are unavailable in demo auth mode. `GET /api/fees?page=0&pendingPage=0&enrollmentPage=0` returns at most 30 obligations with server-calculated balances, capped history for those obligations, receipts, adjustments and submissions. An admin separately pages through 50 pending reviews and 100 enrollment choices at a time. Students see only their student ID. Parents must supply `studentId` from a verified parent-child link. Teachers receive 403 and have no server-mode fee tab. Pending and rejected items are not allocations.

`POST /api/fees` accepts `action: obligation` with `enrollmentId`, `periodKey`, `amountPaise`, `dueOn` and `requestKey`; `action: cash` with `obligationId`, `amountPaise`, `collectedOn` and `requestKey`; `action: adjustment` with `obligationId`, `kind` (`discount` or `waiver`), `amountPaise`, `reason` and `requestKey`; or payer `action: submit` with `obligationId`, `amountPaise`, `transferOn`, `referenceId`, `requestKey` and a required `studentId` for parents. `PATCH /api/fees` accepts `id`, `decision` (`verify` or `reject`) and either `confirmVerification: true` or `reason`. `PUT /api/fees/payee` is admin-only and requires `name`, optional `upiId` and `phone`, `adminPassword` and the previously read `version`. `GET /api/fees/qr?obligationId=...&amountPaise=...` requires payer authorization and an amount within the current balance; `download=1` sets an SVG attachment. The payer supplies no destination. Dates are ISO calendar dates entered for the academy's Asia/Kolkata timezone, not fixed demo dates. HTTP conflicts return 409 and validation failures 400. Idempotency keys reject repeated postings rather than replaying a receipt.

## Local acceptance and production boundary

`npm run test:attendance-postgres` applies repository migrations 0000 through 0008 to disposable PostgreSQL and exercises independent admin, student and parent sessions, partial cash collection, UPI pending/rejected/approved states, concurrent approval and collection, reused references, negative/malformed/excessive amounts, authorization, audit, immutable postings and rollback after a deliberately failed audit insert. It includes authenticated Chromium at 1440 and 390 pixels for obligation entry, cash receipt, payer balance, UPI submission and review, QR download, clipboard success/failure and link inspection. It does not exercise a real banking settlement or a mobile UPI app. Existing demo finance remains demo-only and must never back server-mode balances.

Owner decisions still needed: actual amounts and billing dates, period naming, proration, discount/waiver authorization policy, refunds and reversals, receiving account identity, statutory receipt-number and tax requirements. Production also needs Phase 1 account/recovery acceptance, hosted migration review and approved payee configuration. Before production use, add complete financial history pagination beyond the current bounded 300-record per-page previews, retention and export policy for finance records, and a supervised real-device UPI and payout reconciliation check. No owner policy is implied by the synthetic fixtures.
